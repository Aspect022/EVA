# GENERAL RAG ADVISORY

# [SECTION_COMPLIANCE]
All AI systems should follow general ethical guidelines, including transparency, fairness, and accountability. Adhere to the EU AI Act or similar regional frameworks where applicable.

# [SECTION_RISK_HIGH]
High-risk applications in any domain require human-in-the-loop (HITL) oversight and comprehensive risk assessment before deployment.

# [SECTION_RISK_LOW]
Low-risk applications should maintain basic security standards and monitor for drift or unexpected performance degradation.
