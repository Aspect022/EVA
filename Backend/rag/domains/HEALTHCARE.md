# HEALTHCARE RAG ADVISORY

# [SECTION_COMPLIANCE]
Healthcare data is governed by HIPAA (US), GDPR (EU), and other regional privacy laws. Any model processing PHI (Protected Health Information) must ensure data minimization and encryption at rest and in transit.

# [SECTION_RISK_HIGH]
High-risk healthcare applications (e.g., diagnostic assistance, treatment planning) are strictly advisory. Final decisions must be made by qualified medical professionals. Sensitivity and specificity thresholds must be clearly documented.

# [SECTION_RISK_LOW]
Low-risk healthcare applications (e.g., appointment scheduling optimization) should ensure fairness and non-discrimination in access to services.
