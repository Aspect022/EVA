# INSURANCE RAG ADVISORY

# [SECTION_COMPLIANCE]
Insurance applications must comply with fair pricing regulations and anti-discrimination laws. Model bias in underwriting or claims processing can lead to significant regulatory penalties.

# [SECTION_RISK_HIGH]
High-risk insurance applications (e.g., automated claims approval, personalized premium calculation) require rigorous bias testing across protected attributes (age, gender, ethnicity).

# [SECTION_RISK_LOW]
Low-risk insurance applications (e.g., chatbot for policy information) should focus on accuracy and clarity of information provided to customers.
